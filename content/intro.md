Hi! I'm Hyungu. I'm digging through the full stack of physical intelligence for human–robot interaction, from low-level control all the way up to high-level reasoning.

I'm an undergrad in EE at [Yonsei University](https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/), and currently an undergraduate research intern at the [Visual & Geometric Intelligence Lab](https://jaesik.info/) at SNU, advised by [Jaesik Park](https://jaesik.info/).

Long term, I'm working toward *embodiment-agnostic intelligent collaboration*: humans, agents and robots working together, whatever form they take. I'm tackling the missing pieces one at a time:

- **Memory management**
- **Whole-body control**
- **Multi-agent collaboration**
- **Physics & geometry understanding**

Right now I'm working on how coding agents and VLAs or world action models (WAMs) can work together. This builds on a [planning harness on top of a VLA](/blog/agentbeats/) that won both phases of Berkeley's AgentX–AgentBeats, and [belief-graph supervision for long-horizon manipulation](/blog/behavior-2026/) in the 2026 BEHAVIOR Challenge.

I'm always happy to chat about these ideas, so feel free to [reach out](mailto:jkkhg119@yonsei.ac.kr)!
