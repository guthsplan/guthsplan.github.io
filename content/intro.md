Hi, I'm Hyungu. I study electrical engineering at Yonsei University and I'm a research intern at the Visual & Geometric Intelligence Lab at Seoul National University.

I'm interested in *agents*: systems that reason over long horizons, keep track of what they know, and eventually have to *act* in the physical world. Lately that has meant building the glue between high-level reasoning and low-level control: a planning-and-execution harness on top of a VLA that won both phases of Berkeley's [AgentX–AgentBeats](https://rdi.berkeley.edu/agentx-agentbeats) competition, and belief-graph supervision for long-horizon household manipulation in the [2026 BEHAVIOR Challenge](https://github.com/guthsplan/behavior1k-2026-solution).

Next, I want to figure out where the line between *thinking* and *acting* should sit, whether that ends up as low-level VLAs with better memory or as code-as-policy agents that write their own controllers.
