Hi! I'm Hyungu. I'm digging through the full stack of physical intelligence for human–robot interaction, from low-level control all the way up to high-level reasoning.

I'm an undergrad in EE at Yonsei University, and currently an undergraduate research intern at the Visual & Geometric Intelligence Lab at SNU, advised by Jaesik Park.

Long term, I'm working toward embodiment-agnostic intelligent collaboration: humans, agents and robots working together, whatever form they take. I'm tackling the missing pieces one at a time:

  • Memory management
  • Whole-body control
  • Multi-agent collaboration
  • Physics & geometry understanding

Right now I'm working on how coding agents and VLAs or world action models (WAMs) can work together. This builds on a planning harness on top of a VLA that won both phases of Berkeley's AgentX–AgentBeats, and belief-graph supervision for long-horizon manipulation in the 2026 BEHAVIOR Challenge.

I'm always happy to chat about these ideas, so feel free to reach out!
